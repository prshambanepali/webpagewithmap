📋 Important Guidelines

We are collecting voice recordings for research on identifying a speaker’s geographic origin from speech. Please read the instructions carefully before proceeding.

📌 Instructions:
 1. Select your actual hometown or place of birth. Please be honest — this is crucial for the accuracy of our research.
2. Speak clearly and naturally when recording the given sentence.
3. Use a quiet environment and make sure your microphone is working properly.
4. Do not speak random words or make irrelevant sounds, as it may affect the quality of the dataset.
5. Kindly submit once only. Multiple submissions from the same person will not be accepted.
6. You must record one long paragraph and ten random Nepali sentences. The 'Submit' button will be enabled only after you finish recording all of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of this project?

This project aims to study how speech patterns vary across different regions and how voices may reflect geographic origins. Your contribution helps in building a speech-based research dataset.

How will my voice be used?

Your voice recording will be used for research and educational purposes to train an AI voice model by analyzing regional speech characteristics. All data will be anonymized and securely stored.

How do I get started?

Simply select your actual hometown region, enter your age, and record yourself reading the above short sentence. Then, submit your recordings securely through our platform.
